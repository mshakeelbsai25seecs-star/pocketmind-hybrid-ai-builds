# New hire onboarding guide

Welcome to Acme Corp Security Engineering.

## Timeline

Standard onboarding takes **five business days** and covers account provisioning, VPN setup,
security training, and tool access for NexusAI and the SOC knowledge base.

## Day 1 checklist

- Complete HR paperwork and badge pickup.
- Enroll MFA on the corporate identity provider.
- Read the company overview in the general knowledge folder.

## Day 2–5

- Shadow an analyst on tier-1 triage.
- Index your team runbooks in Knowledge Chat for practice queries.

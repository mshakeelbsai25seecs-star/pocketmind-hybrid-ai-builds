# PocketMind Hybrid AI Employee Onboarding Guide

## What PocketMind Hybrid AI does

PocketMind Hybrid AI lets staff use AI through a private company-approved workflow. In Organization Server Mode, the AI model runs on the company server, not on the employee laptop.

## How to use it

1. Open PocketMind Hybrid AI.
2. Go to **Organization Server**.
3. Confirm the server is connected.
4. Choose the approved company model.
5. Click **Use in Chat**.
6. Ask your question normally.

## Good use cases

- Summarizing internal documents.
- Drafting reports and emails.
- Explaining technical documents.
- Creating first drafts.
- Understanding code or logs.
- Brainstorming policy or process improvements.

## Data rules

Follow company policy. If you are unsure whether a document can be used, ask your manager or IT/security contact before uploading or pasting it.

## Important limitations

AI can make mistakes. Review important answers before using them in client, legal, financial, medical, or operational decisions.

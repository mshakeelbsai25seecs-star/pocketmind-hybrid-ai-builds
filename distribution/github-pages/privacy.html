1. Overview

PocketMind Hybrid AI (“PocketMind”, “we”, “us”) is an offline-first hybrid AI desktop application. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app processes, what stays on your device, and what happens if you optionally connect online providers or an organization server.

2. Information we process

Depending on how you use PocketMind, the app may process:

  • Chat prompts, messages, attachments, and generated outputs
  • Local model files (for example GGUF models) and runtime settings
  • Files and folders you explicitly open for chat, coding, image, or SOC workflows
  • Optional API keys and organization-server connection settings you enter
  • Hardware and diagnostics information used to select local runtimes (CPU/CUDA/Vulkan)
  • Backup/export archives you create
  • Local audit or activity logs if you enable them

3. Local-first processing

By default, PocketMind is designed to run on your Windows device. Local chat, local model management, diagnostics, backups, and most workspace features are processed on-device. We do not require an account to install or use core local features.

Application data is stored in local folders you choose or in the product’s default local data locations on your computer.

4. Optional online and organization services

If you choose to enable cloud AI providers or an organization OpenAI-compatible server, prompts and related content you send may be transmitted to those services according to your configuration. Those third parties process data under their own terms and privacy policies. PocketMind does not control their systems.

API keys you provide are stored locally in an on-device vault for use by the app. Do not share keys publicly.

5. Generative AI content

PocketMind includes generative AI features that can create text, images, code, and related content. Outputs may be inaccurate or incomplete. Review generated content before relying on it, especially for security, legal, medical, or operational decisions.

6. What we do not collect by default

PocketMind does not require cloud telemetry to operate local features. We do not sell personal information. We do not use your local documents or chats to train foundation models for us unless you independently send that content to a third-party service you connect.

7. Microsoft Store and installer delivery

If you obtain PocketMind through Microsoft Store or download an installer from a published package URL, Microsoft and the hosting provider may process standard download, purchase, and device information according to their own policies.

8. Support communications

If you email support, we process the contact details and message content you send so we can respond. We retain support emails only as long as needed to resolve your request and maintain basic business records.

9. Data retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it, uninstall the app, or clear the relevant folders/backups. To remove local data, uninstall PocketMind and delete remaining application data directories if desired.

10. Children’s privacy

PocketMind is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). Do not use the product to knowingly collect children’s data.

11. Security

We design PocketMind with local control in mind, including optional encrypted key storage and local diagnostics. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure. Protect your device, backups, and any API credentials you configure.

12. Your choices

  • Use local models only and leave online providers disabled
  • Choose which folders and files the app may access
  • Enable or disable optional features such as audit logging and backups
  • Disconnect organization servers and remove stored API keys
  • Contact us to ask privacy questions about the product

13. International use

If you enable third-party online services, your content may be processed in regions chosen by those providers. Review their policies before enabling them.

14. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as PocketMind evolves. The effective date above will change when updates are published. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
